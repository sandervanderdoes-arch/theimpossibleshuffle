# Time Traveller merch package

One-of-a-kind print files for every Time Traveller, generated from their own mission: their name,
their Time Traveller number, the mission date and their own best shuffle out of 104 trillion (a deck
order nobody else has, printed with its recipe so anyone can replay it on the site).

**Make a buyer's files:** open `https://theimpossibleshuffle.com/merch/time-traveller/designer.html`
(not linked from the site, not indexed), pick the Time Traveller, check the previews and press
"Download PNG" for each design. Numbers follow the order travellers boarded (hidden test rows don't count).

| File (sample) | Design | Size | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sample-patch.png` | Mission patch: golden-helmet astronaut, "TIME TRAVELLER", "No. 001 · NAME", "66,000,000 years · 8 × H100 · Matches: 0" | 3000 × 3000 | Hoodie or jacket chest, cap side, sticker |
| `sample-best-shuffle.png` | "My best shuffle": all 52 cards of their best shuffle, Grail matches in gold, score, odds, name, recipe | 4500 × 5400 | Tee or hoodie back, poster |
| `sample-route.png` | "I shuffled through all of this": 66 million years from the asteroid to today, shuffle count, "Matches: 0" | 4500 × 5400 | Tee or hoodie back |

| `preview.png` | All four together | | |

- Transparent backgrounds, flat colours (no gradients) for direct-to-garment printing on dark garments.
- Fonts: Bungee and JetBrains Mono, both SIL Open Font License (fine for merch).
- The samples use a made-up name and a real recipe (the sample's best of 30,000 shuffles).
