# Time Traveller merch package

One-of-a-kind print files for every Time Traveller, generated from their own mission: their name,
their Time Traveller number, the mission date and their own best shuffle out of 104 trillion (a deck
order nobody else has, printed with its recipe so anyone can replay it on the site).

**Make a buyer's files:** open `https://theimpossibleshuffle.com/merch/time-traveller/designer.html`
(not linked from the site, not indexed), pick the Time Traveller, check the previews and press
"Download PNG" for each design. Numbers follow the order travellers boarded (hidden test rows don't count).

- Transparent backgrounds, flat colours (no gradients) for direct-to-garment printing on dark garments.
- Fonts: Bungee and JetBrains Mono, both SIL Open Font License (fine for merch).
- The samples use a made-up name and a real recipe (the sample's best of 30,000 shuffles).

## Their own deck of cards

A real deck of 54 playing cards, printed in the exact order of the traveller's best shuffle. It
exists once on Earth. In the designer, scroll to "Their own deck of cards" and press
**Download the whole deck (ZIP)**. `deck-preview.png` shows a sample.

What's in the ZIP (every image 822 × 1122 px: poker size 2.5 × 3.5 in plus 1/8 in bleed at 300 dpi):

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `front-01-…png` to `front-52-…png` | The 52 faces in best-shuffle order. Each says "No. n of 52"; cards in their Holy Grail spot get a gold frame and a "★ In its Holy Grail spot" band |
| `front-53-joker-certificate.png` | Joker 1: "The NAME deck", their score, Time Traveller number, date and recipe |
| `front-54-joker-holy-grail.png` | Joker 2: the full Holy Grail order, with the cards their deck matches in gold |
| `back.png` | One back for all cards: golden astronaut, "Time Traveller", number and name |
| `box-front.png`, `box-back.png` | Tuck box panels |
| `ORDER.txt` | The order as text, plus the recipe (use it to check the printed deck) |

**Order one (MakePlayingCards.com):**

1. Custom game cards → **Poker size (63 × 88 mm)**, 54 cards, **"Different images" for the
   fronts**, the same image for all backs.
2. Upload `front-01` … `front-54` in file-name order (sorted by name they are already in order),
   then `back.png`.
3. Add a custom **tuck box** and use `box-front.png` and `box-back.png`. If the box builder asks for
   a different size, let it fit the image: everything important sits well inside the edges.
4. Ship to the address from the Stripe payment (Payments → the payment → Customer → Shipping).

Expect roughly $15–25 per deck plus shipping, a week or two to arrive. MPC usually keeps the
upload order, but each face carries its number, so it can always be put back in order.
All text stays at least 72 px (about 1/4 in) inside the card edge, so cutting tolerances never clip it.
